Keep putting off your to-dos? Get them done with friends.

Aido is a social to-do app that helps you follow through every day, with AI to plan and friends to keep you going.
Just say what you need to do and it becomes a to-do. With friends by your side, sticking with it gets easier.

[ Invite friends and earn Premium together ]
- Send your invite link on KakaoTalk or any app, or share your 6-character invite code
- Friends who joined within the last 7 days get 7 days of Premium when they accept
- When your friend completes to-dos on 2 days within 14 days of joining, you get 7 days of Premium and can add 1 more friend
- See your paw prints and rewards at a glance in “Collect paw prints”
- Rewards are in-app Premium with no cash value, once per device and account
- Earn up to 30 days of Premium every 30 days, 90 days in total (see Terms of Use)

[ Smart to-dos with AI ]
- Type or say it, and AI picks out the to-do, date, and time
- “Next Friday” or “in 3 days” is worked out from today in your time zone
- Say “work out every Mon, Wed, and Fri” to set up a repeating to-do
- Turn a single note into several to-dos with AI
- Get routine suggestions based on to-dos you repeat (Premium)
- Weekly and monthly AI reports break down your completion rate, categories, and best days (Premium)

[ Stay motivated with friends ]
- Find friends by user tag (#) and see each other’s to-dos in the feed
- Nudge a friend who’s slacking off
- Send a cheer to friends who finish their day

[ See your progress ]
- Check your daily completion rate on weekly and monthly calendars
- Build streaks and stay consistent

[ Stay organized ]
- Sort to-dos with color-coded categories
- Add dates and times to plan your day like a calendar
- Break big to-dos into subtasks
- Set up repeating to-dos for your weekly routine
- Drag and drop to prioritize
- Choose what’s public and what stays private

[ Reminders that fit your day ]
- Morning and evening reminders to start and wrap up your day
- Automatic alerts 60 and 10 minutes before a to-do starts
- Set your own reminder times (Premium)

[ Home Screen widgets ]
- Small, medium, and large sizes show today’s to-dos, progress, and streak
- The large widget adds your week, with paw prints on days you finished it all
- Tap a to-do or date to open it, or “+ Add to-do” to start one
- After midnight, widgets switch to the new day without opening the app

[ Make it yours ]
- Light, dark, or system theme
- 8 cat-themed Premium app icons

[ Made for bigger screens ]
- Unfold iPhone Duo to see your list and details side by side, and pick up right where you left off

[ Easy to start ]
- Sign in with Kakao, Google, Naver, or Apple
- Or sign up with email
- Have an invite code? Enter it on the sign-in screen

Start with today’s to-dos on Aido.

[ Subscription ]

Aido’s core features are free.

Subscribe to Aido Premium to unlock:
- Unlimited AI
- Weekly and monthly AI reports
- Routine suggestions
- Custom reminder times
- Unlimited friends and nudges
- Up to 30 categories
- Premium app icons (8 cat themes)

Premium you earn by inviting friends includes the same features. Rewards earned while you’re subscribed start after your subscription ends.

- Aido Premium is a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription. The price and period are shown in the app before you buy.
- Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
- Your subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- You can manage your subscription in your device settings after purchase.

[ Data sources ]
Aido uses public data from Korea’s Public Data Portal (data.go.kr).

- Korea Meteorological Administration: short-term forecasts, UV index
- AirKorea (Korea Environment Corporation): real-time air quality (PM10, PM2.5)
- Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute: sunrise and sunset times
