Improvements
- Tapping a button twice quickly, or again while a screen is closing after a save, now sends just one request.
- We tightened the wording across the app so messages are shorter and clearer.
- Calendar import now shows a one-line summary of the events, dates, and destination before you start.
- If you have no to-dos yet, you can start a calendar import right from the To-dos tab.

Bug fixes
- Fixed the Complete action on a notification waiting while you were offline and then running later once you reconnected.
- Fixed reminder times that could be off when the app couldn't read your device's time zone.
- Fixed notification text showing AM/PM times even when you chose the 24-hour format.
- Fixed voice notes picking up audio from another note, and “Keep talking” not working after a failed start.
- Fixed a different screen closing if you left while a save was still in progress.
- Fixed screens opened straight from a link not closing after you saved.
- Fixed a tip bubble appearing on top of a sheet and covering its text.
- Fixed editing, pinning, or deleting a memo that was removed on another device failing again and again.
