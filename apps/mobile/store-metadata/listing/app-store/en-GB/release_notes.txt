New
- Added Import from Calendar. In Me > Settings, choose the events on your device's calendars (iCloud, Google, Outlook and others synced to your device) and bring them in as to-dos in one go. Aido only reads your calendar and never changes or deletes anything in it.
- For each calendar, create a new category (using the calendar's name and colour) or connect one you already have, or tap Put all in one category. You can also choose Include times and Share with friends. Sharing starts off.
- If you leave the app while importing, the server keeps going. When it finishes, you see how many to-dos came in and why any were skipped. Within 7 days you can undo the import, which removes only the imported to-dos you haven't completed. The same event is never imported twice, and holiday, birthday and subscribed calendars start unchecked.
- Importing is free. Free covers events up to 30 days ahead, and Premium up to 365 days ahead. The subscription screens now mention this too.

Improvements
- VoiceOver can read the calendar and range choices and the import progress.

Bug fixes
- Fixed Weather asking for location again every time you opened it on Mac.
